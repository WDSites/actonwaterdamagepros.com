Brooks That Start In Westford And End Up In Acton

Westford's Stony Brook begins as Beaver Brook in Boxborough and takes the Stony Brook name after leaving Forge Pond. Nashoba Brook starts in Westford and flows toward the Assabet River, passing through Acton. Butter Brook and Vine Brook feed it. That matters because the same brooks that flood Acton's low spots get their water from Westford's uplands.

Westford's 2007 watershed plan notes that more impervious cover increases flooding frequency and floodplain size, and that the town's capped landfill sits in the Stony Brook floodplain near town wells. For homeowners the lesson is local: new pavement and roofs mean faster runoff into the same brooks.

The Aquifer Under Westford

The same plan says the Stony Brook watershed has 14 public water supply wells and the southern Nashoba Brook watershed has six, and that Westford's primary drinking-water aquifer underlies Stony Brook. The Westford Water Department owns about 304 acres of public land. If your property is near a well or recharge area, spills and floods have extra consequences.

That is one reason we recommend careful handling of contaminated water. Sewage or chemical water should not be dumped on the ground. A crew disposes of it properly, and you should not pour it outside.

Westford Villages, Roads And Housing

Westford has about 24,600 residents and villages including Forge Village, Nabnasset, Graniteville and Parker Village. Interstate 495 and Routes 110 and 225 cross town. The town was incorporated in 1729, so older houses exist beside newer subdivisions.

In 2010 the census counted 7,498 households with an average of 2.93 people. Larger families and finished basements mean more belongings at risk, so lifting and photographing items before a crew arrives helps.

Insurance, Permits And Services

The Massachusetts Division of Insurance says flood damage is generally not covered by standard homeowners policies, and that you should avoid permanent repairs until the insurer inspects. Permit rules vary, so ask the Westford building department whether your project needs a permit.

For wet structure, water mitigation is the starting point. For contaminated water, see flood damage restoration, and for rebuild, water damage repair. The nearby towns of Littleton and Boxborough share the same emergency line.
