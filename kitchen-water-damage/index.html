Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in Acton, MA

Kitchens hide leaks under cabinets, behind dishwashers and below floors. Call the line and we will find the wet areas, dry them and explain the repair.

☎ Call (978) 252-8637

A kitchen has more water sources in a small space than any other room. A sink supply line, a drain trap, a dishwasher hose, an ice maker line and a refrigerator all sit within a few feet of each other. When one leaks, water collects inside cabinets and under flooring where you cannot see it.

The damage is expensive to ignore because kitchens are built from materials that do badly when wet. Particleboard cabinets swell, laminate floors lift, and subfloor under tile can stay damp for weeks. A leak found early is a drying job. A leak found late is often a partial rebuild.

How Kitchen Leaks Happen In Acton Homes

The 2018 hazard mitigation plan, a town planning document, uses census figures of 8,187 housing units, about 10 percent of them built before 1940. Older kitchens often have updated cabinets over very old subfloors and supply lines, and newer ones have engineered products that tolerate water poorly.

The Acton Building Department, at 472 Main Street, takes permit applications online, and a kitchen remodel that follows water damage may need one. Ask before work begins.

First Hour Safety Steps

When water is in your kitchen

Close the shutoff under the sink or the appliance valve. If you cannot, close the main.

Turn off the dishwasher and unplug small appliances only if your hands and the outlet are dry.

Stay away from wet outlets and the stove. Call your utility or 911 if you cannot safely shut off power.

Empty the cabinet base and move food, cookware and electronics out of the wet area.

Water under your sink or floor? Shut the valve and call the 24/7 line.

How We Dry A Kitchen

We remove toe kicks and open the cabinet bases so air can reach the wet surfaces. Standing water is vacuumed. Wet flooring and underlayment are lifted if they cannot dry in place. A drying mat or a few drilled vents can pull moisture from under tile or planks.

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the room, and a moisture meter tracks cabinets, subfloor and wall bases. Drying follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry reference for professional water damage restoration. Cabinets that dry quickly can often stay, while swollen particleboard is replaced.

For the rebuild, water damage repair covers drywall, flooring and paint. If an appliance caused the leak, appliance leak water damage explains that side.

What Changes The Cost

Cost depends on the size of the kitchen, the flooring, the cabinet material and how long the leak ran. A short leak onto tile is a modest job. A long leak under cabinets and into a finished floor is larger. Scope and price are spelled out before a cabinet is touched.

Insurance And Kitchen Leaks

Insurance guidance from the state's Division of Insurance separates sudden interior water damage from gradual damage. A burst supply line is sudden. A slow trap drip over months is gradual. Photograph the cabinet interior and keep any failed parts.

Prevention And Mistakes To Avoid

  • Check under the sink monthly with a flashlight.
  • Replace old rubber supply lines with braided ones.
  • Do not put cabinets back until the base is dry.
  • Do not ignore a musty smell.
