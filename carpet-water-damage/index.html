Carpet Water Damage Restoration in Acton, MA

Wet carpet can sometimes be saved, and sometimes only the pad goes. Call the line and we will extract, measure and tell you which it is.

☎ Call (978) 252-8637

Carpet is a sponge on top of a sponge. The surface fibers dry quickly, but the pad underneath holds water for days, and the subfloor below that can stay damp for longer. A carpet that feels fine after a day of fans may still be growing mold underneath.

Whether carpet can be saved depends on two things. One is the category of the water, meaning how clean or contaminated it was. The other is how long it sat. Clean water caught within a day or two usually leaves carpet worth drying. Contaminated water, or clean water that sat for days, usually does not.

What Decides Whether Carpet Can Be Saved

Acton's 2018 hazard mitigation plan, a town planning document, counts 8,187 housing units, and a large share of Acton homes have wall-to-wall carpet over basements, bedrooms and stairs. Because many carpets sit over concrete slabs or plywood subfloors, the first check is what is under them.

First Hour Safety Steps

When carpet is soaked

Stop the water at its source. Close the valve or the main.

Turn off power to wet outlets only if the panel is dry. Otherwise call your utility or 911.

Do not walk on soaked carpet near outlets, cords or lamps.

Lift furniture off the carpet and put foil or plastic under the legs to prevent stains.

How We Extract And Dry Carpet

We start with extraction using a weighted extraction wand or a truck-mounted unit, which pulls far more water than a household vacuum. If the pad is salvageable it stays, but most pads are removed because they hold water against the subfloor. The carpet is then lifted and propped on air movers so air reaches both sides.

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run in the room, and a moisture meter checks the subfloor and wall bases. Edges are checked for tack strips and baseboards. ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry reference, sets the approach for category and drying decisions.

When carpet is replaced, water damage repair covers flooring and trim. For water that sat on a lower level, see basement water damage cleanup.

What Changes The Cost

The room size, the carpet and pad type, the water category and how long the carpet sat wet are the main factors. Contaminated carpet needs disposal and replacement, while clean water caught early needs extraction and drying. You hear the scope and the price before any carpet is lifted.

Insurance And Carpet

The state Division of Insurance in Massachusetts says that a sudden interior water loss may be handled differently from a gradual problem. Keep a sample of the carpet and pad if you can, and photograph the wet areas before any cutting. Insurers often ask whether the carpet could have been saved.

Mistakes To Avoid

  • Using fans alone and leaving the pad in place.
  • Walking on wet carpet and spreading dirt.
  • Using a household vacuum on soaked carpet.
  • Waiting more than a day or two to start drying.
