Emergency Sewage Backup Cleanup in Acton, MA

Sewage in the house is a health and safety problem first. Keep out of it and call the 24/7 line for extraction, removal of contaminated materials and sanitizing.

☎ Call (978) 252-8637

Sewage backup means wastewater has come back up a drain, toilet or floor drain instead of leaving the house. It is treated as Category 3 water, the most contaminated, because it can carry bacteria and other pathogens. That changes the work. Contaminated porous materials are removed instead of dried, and the crew wears protective gear.

In Acton the cause depends on where your waste goes. A 2019 town release says sewer service covers South Acton, Kelley's Corner and the Acton-Boxborough High School campus, and the rest of town relies on private septic. A homeowner on public sewer faces a blocked main or a surcharge. A homeowner on septic may face a full tank, a failed leach field or a clogged line.

Why Sewage Backs Up Locally

The 2018 hazard mitigation plan, a town document, reports that roads flood about every other year and that freezing followed by rain can block catch basins. Saturated ground raises water around septic systems and can overload lines. The plan also notes that Acton relies solely on subsurface wells, so keeping wastewater out of the ground around a well matters.

First Hour Safety Steps

When sewage is in your home

Stop using water. Do not flush, run the washer or use sinks.

Keep people and pets out of the affected area, especially children.

Do not walk through the water if power or outlets are near. Call your utility or 911 if you cannot shut off power safely.

Avoid skin contact and do not try to clean it yourself with a mop and bleach.

How We Clean Up After A Sewage Backup

The crew wears protective clothing, gloves and respirators. Contaminated liquid is extracted with pumps or extraction units. Porous materials that soaked it up, including carpet, pad, insulation and low drywall, are bagged and removed, because cleaning them cannot make them safe.

Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated with a disinfectant or antimicrobial. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration can run during the work to reduce airborne particles. Then we dry the structure with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers and confirm with meters. ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry reference, sets out the approach for Category 3 water.

The cause itself belongs to a plumber or septic professional. Once the area is safe, water damage repair rebuilds what was removed. For the broader flood context, see flood damage restoration.

What Changes The Cost

The amount of contaminated water, how far it spread, how many materials must be removed and how long it sat all matter. Finished basements with carpet and drywall take more removal than a bare concrete floor. Scope and price are explained up front, before protective gear goes on.

Insurance And Sewage Backup

The Massachusetts Division of Insurance explains that flood and sewer backup are generally excluded from standard homeowners policies unless a rider or endorsement has been added. Check whether your policy has one. Keep photos, receipts and any plumber or septic reports.

Prevention And Mistakes To Avoid

  • Do not put wipes, grease or paper towels down drains.
  • Do not try to dry or save carpet that touched sewage.
  • Have a septic tank pumped on a schedule if you have one.
  • Never mix cleaning chemicals to treat sewage.
