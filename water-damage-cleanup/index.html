Cleanup Is Different From Drying

Mitigation stops the spread and dries the structure. Cleanup deals with what the water left behind: silt, debris, soaked contents and surfaces that touched dirty water. In practice the two overlap, but they are different jobs. If water is still being extracted and dried, that work is described under water mitigation. This page covers what comes after.

What Gets Removed And What Gets Saved

The rule of thumb in S500-based work is that porous materials contaminated by dirty water do not come back. That means carpet pad, insulation, particleboard and soft furnishings that sat in Category 2 or 3 water usually go out. Solid wood, glass, metal and some plastics can be cleaned and kept.

Papers, photos and electronics are special cases. Move them to a dry room right away, and if they are valuable, ask the dispatcher about drying options before throwing them out. Do not power on anything that got wet.

Sanitizing And Deodorizing After Dirty Water

After debris is removed, surfaces are cleaned and treated with an antimicrobial suited to the water category. Odor is addressed at the source by removing the material that holds it, then by cleaning and air filtration. Fogging that only masks smell is not a substitute. If a musty smell lingers after drying, it can point to hidden moisture or mold, and the next step is covered under mold remediation.

Safe cleanup steps you can do before a crew arrives

  • Wear gloves and boots if you must enter wet areas, and avoid contact with sewage or flood water.
  • Stop and fix the water source before moving anything.
  • Open windows for airflow only if outdoor air is dry, and keep HVAC off if the system might be wet.
  • Move undamaged items to a dry room and set aside wet items for the technician to review.
  • Do not mix cleaning chemicals, especially bleach with ammonia.

Acton Conditions That Shape Cleanup

In 2023 a storm that the town's trails page estimated at 4.1 inches of rain flooded the Assabet River trail and canoe launch and damaged a culvert at Camp Acton. Storm water like that is treated as contaminated, so cleanup after a storm is a different job than cleanup after a clean supply-line leak.

Acton also has a large renter population. The town plan reports 24 percent of housing units are renter-occupied. If you rent, tell your landlord about water damage right away and keep the photos you take. Landlords and tenants have different insurance responsibilities, and the lease and policy decide who pays for what.

Cost Factors And Insurance For Cleanup

Cleanup price depends on the water category, the amount of debris and contents, the area affected, how many materials are removed and whether disinfection is needed. We explain the scope and price before work starts. For a number tied to your house, call the line.

The Massachusetts Division of Insurance advises homeowners to photograph damage, keep receipts and wait for the insurer to inspect before permanent repairs. It also says damaged personal property should be kept for the adjuster to see. Whether cleanup is covered depends on your policy and the cause of the water.

When cleanup is finished and the structure is dry, rebuild is handled under water damage repair. If water is still standing, see emergency water removal first.
