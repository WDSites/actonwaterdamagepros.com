Safety Before You Do Anything Else

Before you wade into standing water

Assume anything plugged in could be live. If outlets or appliances are underwater, call your electric utility or 911 before entering.

If the water is from a sewer line or outdoor flooding, avoid skin contact and keep children and pets out.

When Standing Water Is An Emergency

Any water deeper than a puddle that is not draining is an emergency. A burst pipe, a failed sump pump, a water heater rupture or a toilet that will not stop overflowing can put inches of water on a floor in minutes. The goal is to remove the volume first, then dry what remains.

Extraction uses submersible pumps for deep water, truck-mounted or portable extraction units for water in carpet, and wet vacuums for the last film. Once standing water is out, water mitigation takes over with moisture mapping and drying equipment.

Burst Pipe Water Damage

Burst pipes are a winter story in Massachusetts. Acton's hazard mitigation plan gives average annual snowfall of 48 to 72 inches, and freezing temperatures followed by thaw are when supply lines in outside walls and unheated spaces give way. The Massachusetts Division of Insurance notes that frozen-pipe claims may be denied if the damage is due to negligence, such as not keeping the home adequately heated when you can.

If a pipe bursts, shut off the main valve, open a faucet to drain what is left and call the line. Photograph the pipe and the damage before anything is moved. The plumber repairs the pipe, and we remove the water and dry the structure. Do both on the same day if possible, because water in a wall cavity does not wait.

Sump Pumps, Water Heaters And Appliances

In Acton the other common cause is groundwater. The town's plan says roadways flood about every other year and that beaver activity affects nearly every brook, so a wet spring can overwhelm a sump pump that was marginal already. A pump that fails or loses power during a storm is a classic cause of a basement with inches of water.

Water heater failures and washing machine hose bursts tend to release water fast and on the finished floor above the basement. If water has run through a floor into the ceiling below, that ceiling is wet too, and the damage is two floors, not one.

Utility Shutoffs And Who To Call

The Acton Water District serves about 95 percent of the town from eleven wells and says an employee is on call 24 hours a day for emergencies. If the break is in the main or the service line before the meter, that is the right call. If the break is inside the house, the main shutoff and the restoration line are what you need.

Know where your main valve is before you need it. In many homes it is in the basement near the meter, and a quarter turn or a few clockwise turns closes it. Finding it in the dark with water around your ankles is the wrong time to learn.

What Removal Costs And What Insurance Looks At

Cost depends on how much water there is, which category it is, how many rooms are affected, how long extraction and drying take and whether materials need to come out. We explain scope and price before work starts. Insurance decisions follow your policy, so photograph everything, keep receipts for emergency steps and call your insurer or agent promptly.

After the water is out and the structure is drying, the damage is rebuilt under water damage repair. If the water was flood or sewage water, the process follows the safeguards on flood damage restoration.
