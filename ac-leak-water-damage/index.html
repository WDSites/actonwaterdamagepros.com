AC Leak Water Damage Repair in Acton, MA

An AC condensate line that clogs or a coil that drips can stain a ceiling or soak a wall. Call the line and we will dry and repair the damage.

☎ Call (978) 252-8637

An honest note first. Air conditioner leaks are a smaller part of the water damage we see in Acton than basement flooding, roof leaks and burst pipes. Summers here are short compared with winters, and many homes have window units, ductless wall units or no central cooling at all. Where central air is installed, though, the same few failures cause most leaks.

Air conditioners remove moisture from indoor air, and that water has to go somewhere. It drips into a pan or a condensate line, travels to a drain or pump and leaves the house. If the line clogs, the pan rusts through or the coil freezes and thaws, the water takes another path, usually down a wall or through a ceiling.

How AC Leaks Show Up In Local Homes

The 2018 hazard mitigation plan, a town document, counts 8,187 housing units in Acton, and many of them are townhouses, condos and rentals with air handlers in attics or closets above finished rooms. A clogged condensate line in an attic air handler is a classic way to get a brown ceiling stain a few feet away.

First Hour Safety Steps

When your air conditioner is leaking

Turn the system off at the thermostat and the breaker. Running it keeps producing water.

Keep clear of any ceiling that is stained, bulging or dripping near light fixtures.

If water has reached outlets or a panel, call your electric utility or 911 and do not touch the wet area.

Place a bucket under a drip and move furniture and electronics out of the way.

How We Dry And Repair AC Water Damage

We find the extent of the wet area with a moisture meter and a thermal camera. Drywall that is soft or stained all the way through is cut out. Insulation above is checked and removed if wet. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the cavity, and we log the readings against the drying goals in ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry reference.

Because condensate water can carry dust and a film of biological growth from the pan, we clean the surfaces we touch and treat them with an antimicrobial. If the staining is old and you see growth, see mold remediation for how containment works.

Once the structure is dry, water damage repair rebuilds the ceiling or wall, including texture and paint. We explain the scope before anything is cut.

What Changes The Cost

Cost depends on how long the unit leaked, how much drywall and insulation were affected and how hard the area is to reach. A small stain under a bathroom ceiling is simple. A long leak in an attic that soaked insulation over a whole hallway is more involved. You get the repair scope and the price in plain terms before we cut anything.

Insurance And AC Leaks

The Massachusetts Division of Insurance points out that gradual water problems and sudden ones are often handled differently. A clogged line that overflowed overnight is usually sudden. Months of unnoticed dripping is gradual, and the cost of repairing the air conditioner itself is not part of a water claim. Read your policy and ask your insurer.

Prevention And Mistakes To Avoid

  • Flush the condensate line once a season and check the pan.
  • Install a float switch that shuts the system off if the pan fills.
  • Do not ignore a small ceiling stain below an air handler.
  • Do not paint over a stain until the source has been fixed.

If the leak came from a roof or flashing instead of the unit, see roof leak water damage. We cover AC leak calls in Acton and nearby Concord and Westford.
